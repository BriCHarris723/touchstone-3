Our Story

Founded by a team of passionate home bakers, North Star Bakery opened its storefront to serve hand-formed loaves to our neighbors. We believe that good bread takes time, patience, and a lot of love.

Sourcing Philosophy

We source 100% organic flour from regional stone mills. Our seasonal fruits are bought directly from local farmer markets to support community agriculture and ensure absolute freshness.

Staff Highlights

Our head baker, Clara, spent a decade studying traditional French baking methods before bringing her expertise back home. Alongside her is our talented pastry chef, Marcus, who specializes in flaky, laminated doughs.
